Quick answers

  • Where is my data? Only on your phone. KuryentIQ has no account and no server.
  • I got a new phone. In the old app, go to More and tap Make backup. Save the file, move it to your new phone, then restore it in More.
  • The estimate looks different from my bill. It is an estimate. Type the watts from the appliance label and add your real bills in the Bills tab to see how close it is.
