Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

KuryentIQ helps people in the Philippines understand and lower their electricity costs. KuryentIQ does not collect your information. Your records stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Property names, electricity providers, and rates
  • Appliances, their power and usage, and notes
  • Monthly bills and estimates
  • Photos you take of appliance labels
  • Your choice to turn the app lock on or off

KuryentIQ has no server and no account. We do not receive, store, sell, or share any of this.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not use third-party services that receive your data.
  • We do not connect to the internet to run the app.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to photograph or pick an appliance label. The text on the label is read on your phone. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Face ID, fingerprint, or passcode: only if you turn on the app lock. Your phone checks who you are. KuryentIQ never sees your face, fingerprint, or passcode.

Backups

A backup file is created only when you tap "Make backup". You choose where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive). The file is plain text and contains your records, so keep it somewhere private. If you store it with a cloud service, that service's own privacy policy applies to the file.

Purchases

If KuryentIQ offers a one-time purchase, the App Store or Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete a property, appliance, or bill inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your phone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

KuryentIQ is a household tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
